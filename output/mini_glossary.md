#  Mini Glossary

| Term       | Simple Meaning                                                                           | Example                                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dataset    | A collection of related information.                                                     | The synthetic CSV containing our records is a dataset.     |
| Schema     | The blueprint that tells us what fields exist and what type of information they contain. | `record_id` is a field in the dataset.                     |
| Metadata   | Information that describes the data.                                                     | Dataset name, source and version are examples of metadata. |
| Validation | Checking whether the data follows defined rules.                                         | Checking whether quantity is within an acceptable range.   |
| Lineage    | Tracking where data came from and what happened to it.                                   | Tracking a record from its source to the final report.     |
